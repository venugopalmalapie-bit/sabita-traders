Which 40–50 HP John Deere tractor should you buy in Ganjam? 5105 vs 5039D vs 5045D vs 5050D

Start with your work, not the HP. For wet paddy, most Ganjam farmers do best with the 5045D PowerPro 4WD (46 HP), the tractor they buy most from us. Pick the 5105 if budget comes first, the 5039D PowerPro for dry land and PTO work, and the 5050D for the heaviest jobs.

By M Krishna Reddy, Managing Director, Sabita Traders · Published 22 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Ask which model suits my workSee the four side by side

Quick answer

  • Wet paddy and puddling: 5045D PowerPro 4WD (46 HP). It is Ganjam’s most bought: 39 of our 84 tractor sales since June 2022.
  • Tightest budget: 5105 (40 HP), from ₹6.20 lakh after subsidy for the 2WD.
  • Dry land and PTO work: 5039D PowerPro (41 HP). John Deere India lists it as 2WD only.
  • Heaviest work and haulage: 5050D (50 HP), with 1,800 kg lift and a bigger ₹1.5 lakh subsidy.
  • All four: oil-immersed brakes and John Deere’s 5-year / 5,000-hour warranty.

1. Start with your work, not the HP

All four tractors are 40–50 HP John Deere D Series models with 3-cylinder engines and oil-immersed brakes. The right one depends on what you will do most with it:

2. 2WD or 4WD?

  • 4WD also drives the front wheels, so it grips better in wet paddy, puddling and soft land. Four in five of our buyers choose 4WD (our sales, June 2022 – Oct 2026).
  • 2WD costs ₹1.40–1.70 lakh less (MRP) and suits dry land, haulage and trolley work.
  • The 5105, 5045D and 5050D come in both. The 5039D PowerPro is 2WD only.

4. What the extra HP gets you

  • Lift: the 5045D and 5050D lift 1,800 kg; the 5105 and 5039D lift 1,600 kg. That matters for heavier mounted implements.
  • Implements: the John Deere Puddler cum Leveler needs 44 HP or more, so it suits the 5045D or 5050D. The Paddy Special Rotary Tiller works with the 5105, 5045D and 5050D. See all implements.
  • Weight: 1,760 kg for the 5105 and 5039D, 1,850 kg for the 5045D and 1,880 kg for the 5050D (John Deere India figures).

5. The subsidy narrows the gap to 50 HP

Odisha’s tractor subsidy is ₹1.2 lakh up to 48 HP and ₹1.5 lakh for 50 HP and above. So the 5050D gets ₹30,000 more subsidy than the 5045D:

  • 4WD: the MRP gap between the 5045D and 5050D is ₹1.00 lakh; after subsidy it is ₹70,000.
  • 2WD: the MRP gap is ₹70,000; after subsidy it is ₹40,000.

Questions farmers ask us

Which is the best 40–50 HP John Deere tractor for Ganjam?

It depends on your work. For wet paddy, most of our buyers choose the 5045D PowerPro 4WD (46 HP): 39 of our 84 tractor sales since June 2022. For dry land on a budget, the 5105 or 5039D PowerPro; for the heaviest work, the 5050D.

What is the difference between the John Deere 5045D and 5050D?

The 5050D has 50 HP against 46 HP, weighs 1,880 kg against 1,850 kg, and gets ₹1.5 lakh subsidy instead of ₹1.2 lakh. Both lift 1,800 kg and come in 2WD and 4WD. After subsidy, the 4WD 5050D costs ₹70,000 more than the 4WD 5045D.

Is the John Deere 5039D available in 4WD?

John Deere India lists the 5039D PowerPro as a 2WD tractor. For wet paddy work, look at the 5045D PowerPro 4WD or the 5105 4WD.

Which is the cheapest of the four?

The 5105 2WD: ₹6.20 lakh after the Odisha subsidy, ₹7.40 lakh MRP (tractor only, indicative). The subsidy is subject to eligibility and DBT approval.

Do all four have the same warranty?

Yes. All four carry John Deere’s 5-year / 5,000-hour warranty, as long as the tractor is serviced on schedule.
