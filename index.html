Questions farmers ask.

Which John Deere tractor suits paddy and wet land?

The 5045D PowerPro™ 4WD (46 HP) is our best seller in Ganjam. John Deere fits the 4WD version with high-lug tyres for wet land, and also makes a Puddling Special version. We will suggest the right one for your land on a test drive.

Can I take a loan and the Odisha subsidy together?

Yes, under the Odisha DBT farm mechanisation scheme. Start your loan file before booking on the Go-Sugam portal: your bank must issue the delivery order within the 45-day permit window. Our team helps with both.

What warranty comes with a John Deere tractor?

5 years or 5,000 hours, free and transferable, as long as the tractor is serviced on schedule. Accessories and optional attachments are covered separately.

Where can I service my tractor?

At our workshop on NH5 Pathara Byepass, Dura Bahadurpeta, Brahmapur, open every day from 10 AM to 8 PM. WhatsApp 89085 89085 to book.
