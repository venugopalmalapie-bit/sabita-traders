Odisha DBT subsidy: how it works

  1. Register on Krushak Odisha with your Aadhaar, land details and mobile number.
  2. Apply for the machine on the Government's farm-machinery DBT portal. Our team helps you through each step.
  3. If you take a loan, start the loan file before booking. The permit is valid for 45 days in loan cases.
  4. After delivery and verification, the subsidy is paid to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Finance questions

How much down payment do I need?

It depends on the financier, the loan amount they approve and your documents. Try the EMI calculator above, then ask us for quotes from our partners.

Can I get a loan and the Odisha subsidy together?

Yes. Start the loan file before booking. In loan cases the permit is valid for 45 days, and the bank must issue the delivery order within that time.

How long does loan approval take?

It varies by financier and by how complete your documents are. Bring the full document list to avoid delays.

Can I use my old tractor as the down payment?

Yes. Get an indicative value on our Exchange page. We confirm the final price after an on-field inspection and detailed valuation.
