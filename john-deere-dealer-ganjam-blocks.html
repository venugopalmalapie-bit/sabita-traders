Questions farmers ask

Is there a John Deere dealer for my block in Ganjam?

Yes. Sabita Traders, Berhampur, is the only authorised John Deere dealer for all 22 blocks of Ganjam district. WhatsApp or call 89085 89085.

Can I see a John Deere tractor working before I buy?

Yes. We connect you with a John Deere owner near your village so you can watch it work in your kind of soil.

Which John Deere tractor do Ganjam farmers buy most?

The 5045D PowerPro 4WD: 39 of our 84 tractor sales since June 2022. Four in five of our buyers choose 4WD.

How long does a John Deere tractor last?

Of the tractors we sold that are 10 or more years old, 88% were still coming in for service in 2022–23, and 67 had crossed 8,000 engine hours (John Deere records, Oct 2023).

When should I start buying for the paddy season?

Most of our buyers buy in March–April and in July. Start your loan and subsidy paperwork 4–6 weeks before you need the tractor.

Do you service tractors in villages?

Yes. Our workshop is at the Berhampur showroom, and we also come to your village. Book on WhatsApp 89085 89085.

Not sure of your block or tractor?

Send us your village on WhatsApp. We will suggest the tractor for your land and connect you with an owner nearby.
