Questions about choosing a model

Which John Deere tractor suits paddy and wet land?

The 5045D PowerPro™ 4WD (46 HP) is our best seller in Ganjam. John Deere fits the 4WD version with high-lug tyres for wet land, and also makes a Puddling Special version. We will suggest the right one for your land on a test drive.

Is the 5039D available in 4WD?

John Deere India lists the 5039D PowerPro as a 2WD tractor. For wet paddy work, look at the 5045D PowerPro 4WD or the 5105 4WD.

Should I take 2WD or 4WD?

On the 2WD, the engine drives only the two big rear wheels and the front wheels just steer. The 4WD also drives the front wheels for more grip, which helps in wet paddy, puddling and soft land. The 2WD suits dry land, rabi crops, sugarcane haulage and trolley transport.

Do all four models have the same warranty?

Yes. All four carry John Deere’s 5-year / 5,000-hour warranty, free and transferable, as long as the tractor is serviced on schedule.
