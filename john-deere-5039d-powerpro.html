Is the 5039D available in 4WD?

John Deere India lists the 5039D PowerPro as a 2WD tractor. For wet paddy work, see the 5045D PowerPro 4WD or the 5105 4WD.

What does PowerPro mean?

PowerPro models have a dual clutch and dual PTO. The dual clutch lets you stop the tractor without stopping the PTO, so implements such as a rotavator keep running.
