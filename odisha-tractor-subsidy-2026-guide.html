Odisha tractor subsidy 2026: how to apply in Ganjam

Under Odisha’s DBT farm mechanisation scheme, a Ganjam farmer gets ₹1,20,000 off a tractor up to 48 HP, or ₹1,50,000 for 50 HP and above, paid into the farmer’s Aadhaar-linked bank account. You book on the FM-DBT portal while Ganjam has target, choose an enlisted dealer, and finish payment and delivery within the permit’s validity.

By M Krishna Reddy, Managing Director, Sabita Traders · Published 17 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

  • Subsidy: ₹1,20,000 for tractors up to 48 HP; ₹1,50,000 for 50 HP and above (subject to eligibility and DBT approval).
  • Where to apply: register once on Go-Sugam, then book on the FM-DBT portal (fmdbt.agriodisha.nic.in).
  • Permit: valid 30 days if you pay yourself, 45 days with a bank loan.
  • Next tractor target top-ups: 1 Dec and 15 Dec 2026, 1 Jan and 15 Jan 2027. Booking reopens at 3 PM on those days.
  • Payment: the subsidy goes into your Aadhaar-linked bank account after the dealer’s verification.

How much is the Odisha tractor subsidy in 2026?

The subsidy depends on the tractor’s engine power:

Subsidy amounts are set by the Government of Odisha and are paid only after DBT approval. Implements such as rotavators and cultivators have their own subsidy under the same programme.

Who can apply?

Any farmer in Odisha with a valid Farmer ID can apply, whatever the land size. Groups such as Pani Panchayats, women’s self-help groups and cooperatives can apply through a nominated member.

Two rules to know before you apply:

  • You must keep the tractor for 5 years. Selling it earlier can lead to recovery of the subsidy.
  • Tractor-drawn implements are only for tractor owners, one of each type per tractor registration.

What documents do you need?

  • Farmer ID (Krushak Odisha)
  • Aadhaar linked to your Farmer ID
  • Aadhaar linked to your bank account (the subsidy is paid through the Aadhaar-Based Payment System)
  • Your registered mobile number, for the OTPs at every step
  • For a bank loan: a CIBIL score above 700, plus the lender’s usual papers, such as land records, bank statements, PAN and photographs

If your Farmer ID is not linked to Aadhaar, your Block Agriculture Officer can add it.

How to apply, step by step

  1. Register. Register on Krushak Odisha and Go-Sugam (sugam.odisha.gov.in) with your Farmer ID, Aadhaar and mobile number.
  2. Log in to FM-DBT. From 2026-27, tractors are booked and permits issued on the FM-DBT portal (fmdbt.agriodisha.nic.in). Log in with your Farmer ID and Aadhaar and confirm the OTP.
  3. Book while Ganjam has target. Bookings open in phases and are held for 10 days.
  4. Choose make, model and dealer. Select John Deere, your model and Sabita Traders from the dealers listed for Ganjam. We give you an offer price the same day.
  5. Print the permit. It is valid for 30 days if you pay yourself, or 45 days with a bank loan.
  6. Pay and take delivery. Pay online, or your bank issues a delivery order. Delivery is done with Aadhaar verification, within the permit’s validity.
  7. Verification. We upload GPS-tagged photos and the invoice. Some machines are physically inspected.
  8. Subsidy credited. The subsidy is paid into your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

When are new tractor targets released?

Each district, Ganjam included, gets a yearly target for each machine. From the Agriculture Department’s 31 Oct 2026 notice, new tractor targets under the State Pool are uploaded twice a month:

MonthTarget top-up dates
November 20262 Nov and 16 Nov (done)
December 20261 Dec and 15 Dec
January 20271 Jan and 15 Jan

On those days the portal is closed from 2 PM to 3 PM, and tractor booking reopens at 3 PM. Targets can fill quickly, so keep your documents ready and book early.

Can you take the subsidy with a tractor loan?

Yes. Start the loan file before you book, because the bank must issue the delivery order within the 45-day permit. If the loan is late, the permit can lapse and you would have to wait 3 months to re-apply.

We work with John Deere Financial, banks and finance companies. For a tractor loan, your CIBIL score should be above 700. Use our EMI calculator to plan the down payment and instalments.

Target full, permit lapsed or loan rejected? See what to do if you are not eligible for the subsidy.

What if the farmer dies before delivery?

From the procedure posted on the FM-DBT portal on 5 Nov 2026: if the farmer paid but died before the online supply was completed, a legal heir can get the subsidy and the machine. The spouse comes first, unless they authorise another heir by notarised affidavit.

The heir submits the death certificate, the legal heir certificate from the Tahasildar, an NOC affidavit if there are several heirs, and their own Aadhaar, Voter ID, Farmer ID and bank details at the CDAO office. A district committee checks them and the subsidy is paid into the heir’s bank account.

Questions farmers ask us

How much subsidy do I get on a John Deere 5045D?

The 5045D PowerPro is 46 HP, so it falls in the up-to-48 HP band: ₹1,20,000, subject to eligibility and DBT approval.

How much subsidy do I get on a 50 HP tractor like the 5050D?

₹1,50,000. Tractors of 50 HP and above get the higher amount, subject to eligibility and DBT approval.

Where do I apply for the Odisha tractor subsidy?

Register once on Go-Sugam, then book the tractor and get the permit on the FM-DBT portal, fmdbt.agriodisha.nic.in. We can guide you through it.

When is the next tractor booking date in Ganjam?

New tractor targets are uploaded on 1 Dec and 15 Dec 2026, and on 1 Jan and 15 Jan 2027. Booking reopens at 3 PM on those days.

What CIBIL score do I need for a tractor loan?

Your CIBIL score should be above 700. Start the loan file before you book on FM-DBT, so the bank can issue the delivery order within the 45-day permit.

How long is the subsidy permit valid?

30 days if you pay yourself and 45 days if you buy with a bank loan. Finish payment and delivery within that time.

Can I sell the tractor after taking the subsidy?

You must keep it for 5 years. Selling it earlier can lead to recovery of the subsidy.
