FINANCE

How a tractor loan works

Most farmers buy with a loan. The bank or finance company pays the dealer, you pay a down payment, and you repay in instalments. Until the loan is closed, the lender’s name is noted on your tractor’s RC.

  1. 1
    Choose and get a quotation Pick the tractor and implement with the dealer and take a written quotation.
  2. 2
    Apply to a lender John Deere Financial, a bank or an NBFC checks your documents, credit history, land and farm income.
  3. 3
    Sanction and delivery order Once sanctioned, the lender issues a delivery order and pays the dealer. You pay the down payment.
  4. 4
    Take delivery The lender is recorded on the RC as hypothecation: you own and use the tractor; it is security for the loan.
  5. 5
    Repay Pay instalments on time. Farm loans may offer quarterly or half-yearly instalments; ask your lender.
  6. 6
    Close and clear the RC After the last instalment, get the lender’s NOC and apply with Form 35 to remove hypothecation from the RC.
DOCUMENTS USUALLY ASKED FOR
Aadhaar card PAN card Land records Bank statements Photographs Address proof
GOOD TO KNOW
  • Taking the Odisha DBT subsidy with a loan? Start the loan file before booking, because the bank must issue the delivery order within the 45-day permit.
  • Paying on time keeps your credit record clean for future loans.
  • Keep the NOC and loan-closure letter safely.

ODISHA GOVERNMENT · FARM MECHANISATION

Government schemes for Ganjam farmers.

Tractors and implements bought under these schemes are subsidised through Odisha’s DBT farm mechanisation programme. Every district, Ganjam included, gets a yearly target for each machine. Subsidy amounts are set by the Government of Odisha.

ODISHA STATE PLAN Popularisation of Agricultural Implements, Equipment and Diesel Pump Sets The state’s main farm mechanisation scheme: tractors, rotavators, power tillers, combine harvesters and post-harvest machines. Extended by the Odisha Cabinet for 2026-27 to 2030-31.
GOVT OF INDIA · PM-RKVY Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM) Central scheme for implements such as power tillers, rotavators, transplanters, threshers, balers and drones. Higher assistance for SC, ST, small and marginal, and women farmers.
GOVT OF INDIA National Food Security Mission (NFSM) Also funds farm machinery under Odisha’s DBT farm mechanisation programme.
ODISHA · MISSION SHAKTI Machinery for Women’s SHGs Women’s self-help groups can start agri-ventures by buying farm machinery, applying through a nominated member.
